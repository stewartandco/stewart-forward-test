family tstat_trend_both_asymmetric_payoff: 4 sibling(s), cites 8 card(s) — trend_scan selects the look-back window whose linear time-trend slope has the largest absolute t-value, which is exactly the trend-labelling procedure of 3d4db3693d78d717 and is a genuinely different signal construction from a fixed-speed crossover or a channel breakout -- worth one family so the generation is not four variations of one idea. It is run with direction=both so it is symmetric, and with t_min fixed at 2.0 rather than 3.0: generation 2's measured trade-count starvation is direct evidence that the stricter threshold does not produce a testable number of bets, and 831e8c990e96e75e makes the point that Sharpe scales with the number of bets, so throwing away bets to raise precision has a floor. The payoff structure is deliberately asymmetric -- ATR stop at 1.5-2.0 ATR against an r_multiple target of 2-3R -- motivated by 16278b7e95681e44 (filtering to fewer, higher-precision bets improves Sharpe when payoffs are asymmetric) and by the explicit arithmetic of f6bcefd96029682d and a59075409e6a124e, which also warn how fragile that arithmetic is: with a 2:1 payoff, a few points of precision decay wipes out all profit. The r sweep is there to locate that break-even, not to maximise a backtest. vol_percentile again removes top-decile volatility bars so that any surviving edge is not the volatility-level artifact that killed generation 1.
  regime hypothesis: Expects that when a statistically significant linear trend exists over some window of 60-90 days, the next weeks continue it, in either direction. Drift-neutral by construction: the t-statistic is signed, so a significant downtrend produces a short. It is exposed to trend persistence, not to positive drift. Plain statement of the failure mode: this is a trend family, so if realised volatility compresses and 60-90 day slopes stop persisting, the 2-3R targets are rarely reached, the 1.5-2 ATR stops are hit often, and the family loses -- and because the payoff is asymmetric the break-even precision is high (a59075409e6a124e), so it will fail faster than the slower crossover family under the same deterioration. That asymmetry is the point of running it: it is the most sensitive instrument in this generation for detecting whether trend precision, as opposed to trend payoff size, is what has decayed.
