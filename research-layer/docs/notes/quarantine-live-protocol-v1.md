quarantine-live-protocol-v1: The gate governing the quarantine to live transition. PRE-DECLARED BEFORE ANY FORWARD RECORD MATURES. The three strategies in quarantine entered on 2026-08-17 and their first assessment becomes possible on 2026-10-17, when 60 trading days accrue; this note is chained before that date and before any of them has a record long enough to look at. It changes no existing verdict, reopens nothing, and moves no strategy. The 107 graveyarded stay buried and the three in quarantine stay in quarantine until this gate says otherwise.

WHY THIS IS LATE, STATED PLAINLY. pipeline/quarantine.py has told every reader since it was written that the graduation comparison "belongs to the quarantine -> live gate, which is pre-declared in its own chained note". No such note existed. Twelve notes are on this chain and none of them governs this transition. The lifecycle in registry.py has always permitted quarantine to live, DSR_MIN sits in gauntlet.py as a constant retained for this purpose and read by nothing, and protocol-v3 relocated the deflated Sharpe here on the reasoning that this is where forward evidence exists to compute it on. The relocation was recorded; the gate was never specified and never built. This note closes that, and the code's claim becomes true only from this entry onward.

THE FOUNDING OBSERVATION: SIXTY DAYS CAN DISPROVE BUT CANNOT CONFIRM. The probabilistic Sharpe ratio's standard error scales with the square root of the observation count, so at T=60 the confidence interval around a Sharpe estimate is enormous. Clearing 0.95 on a 60-day record requires an annualized Sharpe of about 4.14 even against a benchmark of zero, and about 4.97 against the deflated benchmark protocol-v3 named, computed at the trials count and trial-Sharpe variance actually chained on these three strategies' gauntlet verdicts. The three strategies here have TRAIN Sharpes of 1.3441, 1.3281 and 0.9663. No plausible strategy clears a significance test on sixty days. But sixty days has ample power to detect a book behaving nothing like the one that was modelled. That asymmetry is a fact about sample size rather than a policy preference, and this gate is built around it: at sixty days the gate can only BURY, and graduation requires a materially longer record.

THE KILL ARM. From MIN_TRADING_DAYS onward, a strategy is graveyarded when its forward record falls grossly outside the behaviour the gauntlet predicted for it. The comparison is made against a cone REBUILT AT THE MATCHED FORWARD TRADE COUNT, not against the cone recorded in the gauntlet verdict: quarantine.py's own CONE_CAVEAT already warns that the recorded cone is a terminal-equity distribution over the strategy's FULL trade count and is therefore not comparable to a short forward record. The rebuilt cone is a bootstrap over the strategy's train-window trade contributions drawing exactly as many trades as the strategy has actually closed forward, giving a terminal-equity distribution on the same footing as the observed one. The forward equity series is the mean across the strategy's assets of the rebased per-asset equity already recorded on every quarantine_decision row, which is the same combination rule the engine uses. A strategy is buried when its forward terminal equity falls below the FIRST percentile of that distribution. One percent, not five: burial is terminal and irreversible on this chain, and a gate that buries one strategy in twenty by chance is not one this project should own.

THE KILL ARM CANNOT FIRE ON ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE. Below a minimum of closed forward trades the cone is not built and no burial occurs. This is the mirror image of protocol-v4's rule that a gate passing on the absence of evidence is not a gate: here the gate BURIES, so the same reasoning runs the other way and silence must not condemn. A strategy that trades too rarely to be judged simply stays in quarantine, which is the honest outcome, and it will not graduate either, because a record with no trades cannot clear the graduation arm.

THE GRADUATION ARM: PLAIN PSR, NO DEFLATION. A strategy graduates when the probabilistic Sharpe ratio of its forward daily returns, computed against a benchmark Sharpe of ZERO using its own forward skew and kurtosis and the full count of forward observations, clears the threshold set by the cohort rule below. The deflation by search burden that protocol-v3 relocated here is REMOVED, and the argument is this. A deflated Sharpe answers a specific question: you tried N configurations and kept the maximum, so is the maximum still significant? That correction belongs where the selection happened, and it has already been paid in full at the gauntlet, which is the reason a strategy had to clear six gates to arrive here at all. A quarantine forward record is not a selected maximum. It is a single named hypothesis, chained before the data existed, tested on evidence nobody chose. Deflating it a second time by the trial count that justified skepticism about the TRAIN result counts the same search twice, and the practical consequence was a gate no strategy could ever pass: at the values already chained on these three strategies' gauntlet verdicts, which give an expected-maximum Sharpe of 0.81 annualized, a strategy at a forward Sharpe of 1.30 would have needed 4089 days, over eleven years, and one at 0.97 would have needed 37647 days, a little over a century.

THE COHORT RULE: BENJAMINI-HOCHBERG AT q=0.05. Removing the deflation resolves one burden and exposes another that this pipeline has never priced. Each forward test is a single pre-registered hypothesis, but they are not run one at a time: protocol-v6 promotes every gate passer to quarantine, so a generation can put many strategies under simultaneous test, and testing twenty at 95 percent confidence produces one false graduation on average. That is a distinct burden from the search: the SEARCH burden asks how many configurations were backtested to find this one and is settled at the gauntlet, while CONCURRENT CONFIRMATION asks how many pre-registered forward tests are running at once and had not, before this note, been charged anywhere. At each assessment the eligible strategies form a cohort, their PSR values are converted to p-values, and the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at a false discovery rate of 0.05 determines which graduate. Benjamini-Hochberg rather than Bonferroni because this is a discovery pipeline that expects several genuine survivors and cares about the proportion of false graduates among graduates, not about avoiding any single false positive at any cost. The procedure adapts: where the whole cohort is strong the weakest graduate still faces 0.95, and where one strategy stands alone among many it faces 1 minus q over m, which is Bonferroni's bar.

THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT PROTOCOL-V6. v6's principle, in Coen's words, is that every individual edge must be standalone WHEN RUNNING THROUGH THE GAUNTLET. It is scoped to that stage and this gate is a later one. No gauntlet gate reads a cohort, and none will; the gauntlet still judges each edge on its own evidence alone. What this note adds is that the confirmation stage knows how many confirmations are running concurrently, which is not a property of any strategy and does not change any strategy's own record.

REACHABILITY, STATED BEFORE A SINGLE RECORD MATURES, SO IT CANNOT BE DISCOVERED LATER AND PRESENTED AS A SURPRISE. Days of forward record required, by forward annualized Sharpe: at a threshold of 0.95, which is what the weakest member of a uniformly strong cohort faces, a Sharpe of 1.0 needs 990 days, 1.3 needs 587, 1.5 needs 442 and 2.0 needs 250. For a lone graduate in a cohort of ten those become 2427, 1438, 1081 and 610 days; in a cohort of twenty, 2881, 1707, 1284 and 724. GRADUATION IS A MULTI-YEAR PROPOSITION AND NOTHING WILL GO LIVE SOON. A strategy holding its train Sharpe of 1.34 needs roughly a year and a half in the best case and closer to four years if it is the only one of its cohort that works. This is recorded here because a gate whose horizon is discovered after the fact invites pressure to move it, and because the pipeline's product claim is its honesty rather than its speed.

A CONSEQUENCE OF PROTOCOL-V6 THAT WAS NOT VISIBLE WHEN V6 WAS CHOSEN. Because the cohort correction scales with how many strategies are under simultaneous test, and because v6 promotes every gate passer instead of one per sibling group, promoting more strategies makes graduation slower for every strategy in the cohort, including the ones that genuinely work. That is a real cost of v6's principle rather than an argument against it, and it is named here so that a later reader does not mistake a slow graduation record for weak strategies when it may be a large cohort.

THE THREE ALREADY IN QUARANTINE CARRY A SELECTION EVENT THEIR SUCCESSORS WILL NOT. The argument for removing the deflation rests on a forward record being an unselected, pre-registered test. That is exactly true of everything entering quarantine under protocol-v6, where nothing is selected and every gate passer is promoted. It is less true of the three already here: they entered under protocol-v3, which promoted the single highest deflated Sharpe per sibling group, so each of them is the survivor of a choice among ten gauntlet passers and seven of their peers were graveyarded as sibling_not_selected. The same gate is applied to them regardless, for the same reason the quarantine-standard-asymmetry note gave when it left them under v3's verdicts: re-judging a closed decision under a later standard is the failure this chain exists to prevent, and it does not become acceptable when the later standard is stricter either. Their group context, trials count and deflated Sharpe are all already on the chain in their gauntlet verdicts for any reader who wishes to weigh their entry differently.

WHAT LIVE MEANS AND WHAT THIS NOTE DOES NOT AUTHORISE. live is a lifecycle state recording that a strategy's forward record cleared this gate. It is not a deployment, not a position, and not an instruction to commit capital. Nothing in this note authorises money at risk; that decision is Coen's, is separate, and has its own requirements that this chain does not attempt to encode.

RATCHET POSITION. Measured against what was previously declared, this note LOOSENS: protocol-v3 named a deflated Sharpe at this gate and this note removes the deflation. It is declared as a loosening and carries the burden. The evidence is arithmetic rather than measurement, computed from values already chained on the strategies' own gauntlet verdicts and from the standard PSR formula, and it is set out above in full and reproduced by a write-free diagnostic committed alongside this note, so there is nothing here that could have been selected to fit a conclusion. It is chained before any forward record matures, which is the only sense in which timing could matter. In the other direction the same note TIGHTENS twice, by adding a kill arm where none existed and by charging a concurrent-confirmation burden that was never charged anywhere; neither tightening requires this justification but both are recorded so the net effect is legible. The standing rule is unchanged: a future protocol may tighten freely, and any loosening requires the evidence and the argument on this chain before the results it would affect exist.
