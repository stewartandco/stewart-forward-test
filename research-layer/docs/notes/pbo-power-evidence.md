pbo-power-evidence: EVIDENCE ONLY. It records what protocol-v5's PBO gate can detect and what generation 4's families actually offered it, and it proposes nothing. No threshold is changed, no gate is amended, no successor protocol is drafted here, and no verdict on this chain is disturbed. The protocol-v5 note chained at registry entry 2512 stands exactly as written; every strategy stays where it is. This is published for the same reason the generation-4 breakdown at entry 2503 and the validity evidence at entry 2511 were: so that when a change is eventually argued for, the measurements behind it are already on the record and cannot have been chosen to fit the conclusion.

WHY THIS WAS MEASURED. The protocol-v5 note names this as its own biggest weakness, in its own words: it is entirely possible that no family this Composer produces will ever clear the gate, and no evidence on this chain establishes that a real family can clear a calibrated PBO test at all. That is a question about the gate's POWER, and it is answerable without running a generation and without touching the holdout.

METHOD. Synthetic families are built to mirror the real ones: a common market factor taken from generation 4's symmetric_tstat_trend_ff, plus idiosyncratic noise at the residual standard deviation measured in that same family, giving a common-factor sd of 0.00472, an idiosyncratic sd of 0.00179 and a total of 0.00505. Into each family an edge is PLANTED: one configuration receives a persistent daily drift sized so that its annualized Sharpe advantage over its siblings equals a stated number, where a 1.0 advantage is 0.000264 of extra daily mean return. That is precisely the situation the gate exists to reward, a family in which selecting the in-sample best genuinely generalises. Each family is then judged exactly as protocol-v5 judges one: CSCV at S=16 over all 12870 splits on the train window with the boundary tie as a half event, compared against that family's own permutation null, passing at or below the 5th percentile. Five independent families per cell give a detection rate rather than a single draw. The diagnostic is diagnose_pbo_power.py, write-free by construction.

WHAT THE GATE DETECTS, at five configurations, five families per cell, fifty-draw nulls. At a planted advantage of 0.00 the observed PBO averaged 0.617 and sat at the 62nd percentile of its own null, detected 1 of 5. At 0.25, PBO 0.474 at the 47th percentile, detected 0 of 5. At 0.50, PBO 0.109 at the 6th percentile, detected 3 of 5. At 0.75, PBO 0.005 at the 0th percentile, detected 5 of 5. At 1.00 and at 2.00, detected 5 of 5. At twelve configurations a 0.50 advantage was detected 4 of 5 and a 1.00 advantage 5 of 5, so a larger family helps, modestly. The single detection at a planted advantage of ZERO is the gate's false-positive rate behaving as designed rather than a defect: at a true 5 percent level the chance of at least one pass in five trials is about 23 percent.

THE GATE IS THEREFORE NOT DECORATIVE. It was reasonable to fear, from the protocol-v5 note's own warning, that a test demanding the 5th percentile of a distribution with a standard deviation near 0.24 would forbid everything. It does not. A persistent advantage of 0.75 annualized Sharpe is detected every time and one of 0.50 most of the time. The gate rewards a real edge at an effect size that is large but not absurd.

WHAT GENERATION 4 ACTUALLY OFFERED IT. The quantity the gate must resolve is the winner's margin over its NEAREST RIVAL, not the family's best-worst spread, because the ranking that CSCV performs is decided at the top of the family. Measured on the committed train-window curves, those margins were: breakout_vol_state_filter 0.000, symmetric_tstat_trend_voltarget 0.010, below_ma_short_regime_trend 0.058, short_only_fast_macross_high_n 0.059, symmetric_tstat_trend_ff 0.097, symmetric_macross_stop_geometry 0.103. The corresponding best-worst spreads, which flatter the families and are recorded only for context, were 0.055, 0.364, 0.161, 0.212, 0.392 and 0.263. So the gate needs roughly 0.50 and the best real family supplied 0.103. The shortfall is a factor of five at its narrowest and unbounded at its widest, since one family's margin is exactly zero.

THE READING THIS FORCES. Generation 4's families did not fail the PBO gate because the gate was set too high. They failed because there was almost nothing to select between, and the gate said so correctly. When the best configuration in a family beats its nearest rival by one hundredth of a Sharpe point, choosing between them IS arbitrary, and a statistic asking whether that choice generalises is answering the question it was asked. No threshold could repair this without making the gate stop reporting a true thing.

AN OBSERVED TENSION BETWEEN TWO GATES, RECORDED WITHOUT A PROPOSED REMEDY. The plateau gate added by protocol-v4 rewards a FLAT neighbourhood: a candidate qualifies only if it and every one-step neighbour score at least 90 percent of the family best, on the stated reasoning that a lone peak surrounded by cliffs is the signature of overfitting rather than of edge. The PBO gate rewards a SEPARABLE one, because on a flat neighbourhood the in-sample winner is chosen by noise and does not persist out of sample. A dense sweep of one parameter is flat by construction, which is what the dense block types were introduced to guarantee. These two gates were adopted in successive protocols and, on the object they are both applied to, they pull in opposite directions. It is not a strict contradiction: a family holding a plateau of genuinely distinct good configurations alongside genuinely worse ones would satisfy both, since the in-sample winner would come from the plateau and still outrank the poor members out of sample. Generation 4 produced no family of that shape, and nothing in the Composer's instructions asks for one. This is recorded as an observation about the gate stack, not as an argument for changing either gate.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH. It is a LOWER BOUND on what the gate demands, not an estimate: the planted edge is uniform and persistent, the most detectable shape an edge can take, so a regime-dependent or episodic edge of the same average size would need to be larger still. Five repeats per cell fixes the shape of the detection curve but not its exact rates, and a fifty-draw null resolves the 5th percentile coarsely. It does not establish that no family this Composer could ever build reaches a 0.50 margin, only that none of generation 4's six came within a factor of five. It does not establish anything about the plateau gate's own thresholds, which have still never fired on real data. And it is not evidence that any particular strategy on this chain was wrongly buried: the nine strategies that died at the PBO gate in generation 4 sat at the 62nd and 72nd percentile of their own nulls, and nothing measured here moves them.

RATCHET POSITION. This note changes nothing and therefore carries no ratchet burden of its own. It is filed in advance of any argument that might cite it, so that a later proposal to move a threshold, to redefine what a sibling group is, or to change what the PBO gate is computed over, must be argued against measurements that already existed before the proposal did.
