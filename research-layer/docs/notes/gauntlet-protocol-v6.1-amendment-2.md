gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-amendment-2: Amends gauntlet-protocol-v6.1, chained at registry entry 68486, and gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-amendment-1, chained at registry entry 81246, in one thing the gauntlet_stats entries record: how the effective trials count is made. From this note onward it is counted per cell partition, by one average-linkage tree per partition cut at a fixed correlation of 0.9, and the partitions' counts are summed. Everything else in v6.1 and amendment-1 stands as written: the floor on trials_n, the floor on SR*, the series the deflated Sharpe is computed on, PBO, plateau_ok and the haircut formula. No gate is added, removed or re-tuned, no threshold moves, and no verdict or state change is touched. Pre-declared before the first per-cell gauntlet_stats entry exists: pipeline/gauntlet_stats.py refuses to chain any gauntlet_stats entry until this note is on the chain.

WHY. Until this note the statistics job clustered every registered strategy in one pass and picked k by the silhouette argmax. That pass holds one n x n float64 matrix over all n registered strategies, and the job starts it only when free physical memory covers it. n grows by hundreds of registrations a day, so the pass would stop fitting within weeks. A memory cut of the same method that gives bit-identical results is not possible: a row-blocked distance matrix differs in the last bit and changes the merge history (measured 2026-11-01). So the count itself changes. Per partition, the matrix held at any time is the largest partition's, not the whole registry's.

THE METHOD. Step 1: the aligned series are built exactly as before (intersection when asset classes are mixed or calendars are ragged, native otherwise): the same rows, the same days, the same length L. Step 2: the rows are split into partitions by cell key, the strategy's cells sorted and joined by "|". A multi-asset strategy (the legacy BTCUSD and ETHUSD pair) is its own partition, and an intraday strategy's key carries its timeframe (BTCUSDT@1h), so an intraday strategy and its daily original sit in different partitions. Step 3: in each partition, the rows that never trade in the aligned window (every value exactly 0.0) are taken out before the tree and form ONE cluster of their own, when the partition has any; its representative Sharpe is 0.0. A row that is constant but not zero makes the whole run refuse: exit 1, exit_reason cluster_refused, nothing chained. Step 4: the remaining rows of each partition get the merge history of the unchanged clustering code (pipeline/cluster.py: the same correlation distance, the same average linkage, the same deterministic tie-break), and each merge's height is read: the average-linkage distance between the two clusters at the moment they merge. Step 5: each partition's tree is cut at the distance t = sqrt(0.5 x (1 - 0.9)), the distance that corresponds to a correlation of 0.9. A merge counts when its height rounded to 12 decimals is at or under t rounded to 12 decimals. The counted merges are the merges in order up to the first one over t; a later merge at or under t (float noise) does not count, and the run reports how many there were as cut_late_merges, in its status file and run log only. A partition's k is its remaining rows minus its counted merges, plus 1 when it has never-traded rows; a partition of one strategy has k = 1. Step 6: trials_n_raw is the sum of the partitions' k. Step 7: the cluster labels are made global by taking the partitions in sorted key order and offsetting each partition's labels by the k of the partitions before it. Step 8: trials_sr_var is the sample variance of every cluster's representative Sharpe (the mean daily Sharpe of its members) pooled over all partitions in one sample; the never-traded cluster of a partition enters it once. Step 9: the floors, SR*, the deflated Sharpe and the haircut are computed by the unchanged code from steps 6 and 8: trials_n is the larger of trials_n_raw and the highest cluster count on the chain, and SR* is floored as amendment-1 says. The deflated Sharpe is still computed on the registry-wide aligned series. The silhouette search is no longer run by the statistics job. The correlation 0.9 is a named constant, CUT_RHO, recorded on every entry; CUT_RHO changes only by a new chained note, never as a tuning knob.

WHAT EACH ENTRY RECORDS. cluster_method is "effective_trials/v4 per-cell average-linkage cut rho>=0.9" (it was "effective_trials/v3 correlation-distance"). Three fields are added: trials_partitions (the number of partitions), trials_largest_partition_n (the strategy count of the largest one) and trials_cut_rho (0.9). Every other field is recorded as before. The per-partition table goes to the job's report file and run log, never onto an entry. The trials_n floor still reads trials_n_raw from earlier gauntlet_stats entries and the cluster counts of v3 to v6 verdicts; the list of protocols it reads is unchanged.

MEASURED. On a copy of the chain read on 2026-11-05, re-statting the 300 verdicts the 2026-11-05 01:00 run statted, on the same registry (30,140 registered strategies), the same data vintage (2026-11-01) and the same data, with nothing chained: 111 partitions, 36 of them intraday; the largest is IWM with 1,068 strategies; L is 1,003 days. The per-cell count gives trials_n_raw 6,183, trials_sr_var 0.001293 and SR* 0.13453. The registry-wide method on the same inputs that night recorded k 4,320, trials_sr_var 0.0011789 and SR* 0.12533, which are also the highest values on the chain, so both floors sit below the per-cell figures and an entry would record the per-cell ones. There were 375 never-traded rows in 15 partitions, no constant nonzero row and no late merge. An independent average-linkage implementation (scipy) cut at the same distance gave the same k in all 111 partitions. Every one of the 300 recorded deflated Sharpe values falls: the median from 1.42e-5 to 3.83e-6. None is at or above 0.95, before or after. The clustering added 332 MB of memory, where the registry-wide pass needed 9,831 MB by the job's own estimate.

RATCHET POSITION. No gate reads trials_n, SR*, the deflated Sharpe, PBO, plateau_ok or the haircut, under v6.1, amendment-1 or here, so nothing a gate decides changes. A higher count and a higher SR* can only lower the recorded deflated Sharpe and raise the recorded haircut: stricter, never more lenient. The rise in the count is a ratchet. Once a per-cell entry is chained, the floors on trials_n and SR* hold its values, so a later return of the code to the registry-wide method cannot lower the recorded trials_n or SR*: a rollback restores the code, not the number. A fixed cut is not strictly monotone as strategies arrive (a new member can pull two clusters under t), so a night's trials_n_raw can dip a little; the floor absorbs the dip, as it does today. Every gauntlet_stats entry chained before this note stands unchanged and is not restated: 7,687 of them on the 2026-11-05 copy, recorded under the registry-wide method, plus any chained after that copy and before this note.

REFUSAL. pipeline/gauntlet_stats.py refuses to chain (exit 1, exit_reason refused_no_amendment2_note) until this note is on the chain, detected by the prefix gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-amendment-2: of its first line, first occurrence. The gauntlet worker's refusal check, the rollback gauntlet's family-kill switch and verify_registry.py invariant 12 detect the v6.1 addendum by the prefix gauntlet-protocol-v6.1: with the colon straight after v6.1; this note has a hyphen in that place, so it arms none of them. Runs without --chain are unaffected.
