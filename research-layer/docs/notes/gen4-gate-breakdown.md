generation-4 per-gate breakdown: PUBLISHED BEFORE ANY THRESHOLD IS DISCUSSED AND BEFORE ANY SUCCESSOR PROTOCOL IS DRAFTED. The gauntlet-protocol-v4 note pre-committed that if generation 4 produced no survivors, a per-gate breakdown of what died where would be published on this chain first, so that the determination of whether the gates caught something real or a threshold was simply set too high would be made from the published evidence rather than from whichever answer turned out to be convenient once the numbers were known. Generation 4 produced no survivors. This note discharges that promise. It reports what died where and states what the evidence does and does not establish. It proposes no threshold change and drafts no successor protocol.

FINDING 1: THE ZERO IS THE PBO GATE, AND IT IS NOT A NARROW MISS. Six gates precede pbo in FAIL_ORDER, so a specification recorded as failing pbo passed all six, and those six include every gate protocol-v3 had. The 9 specifications that died on pbo therefore satisfied the entire protocol-v3 battery: positive out-of-sample edge, edge decay within tolerance with eight of the nine improving out of sample rather than decaying and the ninth at negative 20.5 percent, Monte Carlo P05 between 1.30 and 5.32 against a floor of 1.0, probability of ruin between 0.000 and 0.036 against a maximum of 0.05, and positive net profit under the cost stress. Their deflated Sharpe values, which protocol-v3 used to rank siblings, run from 0.808 to 0.983. Under protocol-v3 all 9 would have passed the gauntlet and its one-slot-per-group selection would have sent two of them to quarantine: b476048b2df2ab6e at a deflated Sharpe of 0.983 and 0022247749b387eb at 0.978. They died instead at group PBO values of 0.689 and 0.803 against a pass line of 0.20 and a family-kill line of 0.50. That is not a threshold set marginally too high. It is a gate reporting that selection among those siblings is worse than a coin flip at picking the configuration that performs out of sample.

FINDING 2: THE SHARPE FLOOR BOUND, AND THIS PROTOCOL PREDICTED IT WOULD NOT. The protocol-v4 note adopted sharpe_floor while stating it was knowingly non-binding, reasoning that all 43 strategies that had ever reached the gauntlet scored at least 0.577 on the train window and that all 24 sub-0.4 specifications had already died at the screen. That prediction was wrong on the first generation judged under it: 9 of the 19 gauntlet-reachers had train Sharpe between 0.043 and 0.209, and the floor rather than the screen is what stopped them. The mechanism is visible and is not a defect in either gate. The screen admits on trade count and net profit and selects for neither Sharpe nor any ratio of return to variability, and generation 4 was the first generation to contain families designed explicitly to maximise trade count. short_only_fast_macross_high_n was proposed to attack the sample-size failure that killed generation 2's short family, and it succeeded at exactly that, clearing the 40-trade floor with 49 trades at a Sharpe of 0.13. The floor was adopted for textual parity between two pipelines and turned out to be the gate that catches a frequency-optimised family. The note also stated that this gate would bite if the screen were ever loosened. What actually happened is that the screen did not move and the population moved instead.

FINDING 3: THE PLATEAU RULE STILL HAS NO EVIDENCE. plateau is last in FAIL_ORDER and no specification reached it, so the gate this protocol exists for did not fire once, and select_survivors ran over an empty set. The protocol-v4 note recorded as a known gap that the plateau ratio of 0.9, the both-sides neighbour requirement and the 0.20 PBO threshold were uncalibrated, and that the write-free ratchet diagnostic could not calibrate them because no strategy then on the chain used a dense block type. Generation 4 closed part of that gap and not the part that was expected. It produced real evidence on sharpe_floor and on PBO and none whatsoever on the plateau rule. The geometry was in place for it: all six groups swept a dense axis at five contiguous values, which under the both-sides rule leaves the three interior points of each group eligible, 18 of the 30 registered specifications in total. Every one of those 18 was eliminated by an earlier gate. The plateau rule remains untested against real data after the generation that was meant to test it.

WHAT THIS BREAKDOWN DOES NOT ESTABLISH. It does not establish that the 9 pbo deaths were false discoveries, only that CSCV over their own sibling matrices reports that selection among them does not generalise; PBO is a statement about a selection procedure applied to a family, not a verdict on any single configuration's economics. It does not establish that 0.20 and 0.50 are the right lines, because a generation whose observed group values are 0.030, 0.651, 0.689, 0.761, 0.803 and 0.873 contains no observation anywhere near either line and therefore carries almost no information about where either line should sit. It does not establish anything about the plateau rule, for the reason given above. And it does not establish that generation 4's families were poor material: two of them held train Sharpe above 0.8 with out-of-sample edge intact, which is the strongest raw material this pipeline has produced, and they were rejected by a gate measuring a different property than the gates that admired them.

ALSO RECORDED, as provenance rather than as findings. The batch-gate drift for this generation was total. The batch approved at the dry-run gate was 49 specifications in 6 families and the batch chained by the real run was 30 specifications in 6 entirely different families, with no family name in common. Generation 3's drift was 20 to 24 with families surviving. Both figures are in logs/batch_drift.jsonl. The dry-run gate is therefore a check on whether the Composer is producing sane families and is not a gate on specific specifications, and it cannot be one while the real run issues a fresh non-deterministic call. Separately, the screen metric net_pnl is final combined equity less one and marks an open position to market at the fence, while the trades and win_rate metrics count closed trades only. breakout_vol_state_filter reported a 0.00 win rate alongside a net_pnl of +1.017 for that reason: 7 closed losing trades, and one position opened after 2020-09-03 that never closed. It changed no verdict this generation, because those specifications failed trade_count first and because every other specification's open-position contribution lay between 0.000 and negative 0.041. It is recorded here because a public net profit figure that can include unrealised marks should not have to be inferred from the code.

THE RATCHET IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOT BEING TESTED BY THIS NOTE. protocol-v4 may be tightened freely. Any loosening requires the evidence and the argument on this chain before the results it would affect exist, and the results generation 4 would affect now exist. This note is the evidence half of that requirement and deliberately contains no argument, because the promise was that the breakdown would be published before any threshold was discussed, and a breakdown that arrived carrying a proposal would not have been that.
