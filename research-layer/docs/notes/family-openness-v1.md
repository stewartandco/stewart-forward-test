family-openness-v1: The protocol governing which PROPOSED VARIATIONS may reach the gauntlet. It supersedes no earlier note of its own kind, because none exists: this is the first chained note about the composer's admission rules rather than about the gate battery. It is not therefore a first statement on the subject. It MODIFIES behaviour that has been in force since the composer was written and that five chained notes have asserted, and it says so plainly rather than resting on the absence of a note named after it. Pre-declared before any re-trial registration exists and before any queue entry exists: verified at source on 2026-08-29, no re-trial code has shipped, composer.registered_fingerprints still maps every strategy_registered entry in any lifecycle state, screen_siblings still drops any composition matching one of them permanently, and validate_family still refuses a family whose sweep exceeds the sibling cap rather than clipping it. Nothing on the chain is re-judged, no buried strategy is unburied, and no verdict moves.

WHAT IS ON THE CHAIN ALREADY, QUOTED, BECAUSE THE SPEC THIS IMPLEMENTS GOT IT WRONG. The SP5 design says the permanent exclusion is not a chained rule. It is. gauntlet-protocol-v2 states that composition fingerprints "block re-registration of any previously registered composition in any state, including duplicate siblings within one family, so no strategy returns under a fresh id". protocol-v3, protocol-v4, protocol-v5 and protocol-v6 each carry it forward in their opening paragraph: the graveyarded remain buried, "the composition-fingerprint guard still blocks re-registration of any of them". So the rule this note changes has been re-affirmed at every protocol change since 2026-08-15, most recently by v6. That makes the pre-declaration requirement stronger rather than weaker, and it is the reason this note is chained before the code rather than alongside it.

WHAT IS UNTOUCHED, STATED FIRST SO IT CANNOT BE READ AS SOMETHING ELSE. Every graveyard verdict stands, unedited, forever. Every buried strategy stays buried, which is terminal, and that expressly includes the 7 buried as sibling_not_selected under protocol-v3 that v6 would not bury today. No strategy_id ever returns. The lifecycle state machine gains no transition and terminal states are not amended. What changes is narrower than any of that: a buried COMPOSITION, which is a fingerprint over universe and blocks and params, may be proposed again as a NEW strategy with a new id and a new number, carrying none of the old one's record. The clause of v2 that this contradicts is the trailing "so no strategy returns under a fresh id", and only that clause, only for future runs.

RE-TRIALS, THE RULE (D9). A composition whose fingerprint matches a registered strategy is dropped as today UNLESS the matching registration is BURIED and that burying verdict's cutoff is at least 6 months behind the target cell's current data end. Past that window the composition re-registers as a new strategy. A declared protocol event, specifically the unified re-run of the whole registry that D8 pre-declares, is the second door and opens it for every buried composition at once. Where the matching registration is NOT buried, in quarantine or live, there is no burying verdict, no expiry, and the composition stays permanently excluded: the strategy is currently under test and a second copy of it is a duplicate, not a re-trial. That resolution is declared here because the rule's wording does not reach the case and the implementation needs an answer.

WHY SIX MONTHS OF NEW DATA AND NOT A CALENDAR TIMER. The gauntlet is deterministic. Given the same spec over the same bars it returns the same verdict, so a same-data re-test is a KNOWN ANSWER bought at the price of a higher bar for every live survivor, which is the worst trade this pipeline can make: it spends the denominator and learns nothing. A re-test after the cell's data has moved on is a different object. The bars are different, the out-of-sample window is different, and the question "does this composition work" has a genuinely different answer available. The clock therefore runs on the DATA, not on the wall, and it runs per cell, because a cell is the unit of survival here.

IN-RUN AND IN-CYCLE DUPLICATES REMAIN MALFORMED AND ARE NOT RE-TRIALS. Two siblings in one family that are the same composition still kill the family as a malformed proposal, and a composition duplicating one already registered earlier in the same run is still dropped. Those ARE same-data by construction, so the paragraph above condemns them directly. Nothing in this note softens either.

CAPS BECOME QUEUES (D10). validate_family's "exceeds cap, rejected, not clipped" refusal is replaced by split-and-carry: a family whose sweep exceeds the per-cycle sibling bound registers the first window now and QUEUES the remainder, persisted in loop state and visible in pipeline_status.json, draining across subsequent cycles until every proposed variation has been tested. The same treatment applies to any other per-cycle bound in this design; the 12-asset rotation was already a schedule rather than a filter and is unchanged.

THE INVARIANT THAT MAKES THAT SAFE, AND IT IS CHECKABLE RATHER THAN ASPIRATIONAL. No proposed variation is ever dropped without either a gauntlet verdict or a queue entry. The gauntlet is the only place an edge can die. A refusal that discards work silently is the one outcome this replaces, because a cap is a capacity limit presented as a judgment, and the pipeline has made that mistake before: protocol-v6 retired the one-winner-per-sibling-group rule for being exactly this, a capacity decision dressed as a statistical one.

THE COMPENSATING DISCIPLINE, WHICH IS NOT OPTIONAL AND IS NOT FREE. Every re-trial and every queued sibling is a NEW NUMBERED TRIAL. It is a strategy_registered entry, so it enters registered_n and the effective-trials clustering that trials_n computes over every registered strategy including every buried one, and the deflated Sharpe on every verdict computed after it is harder to clear. If it survives the gauntlet it joins the quarantine cohort, where the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure divides q by the cohort size, so it also raises the graduation bar for every strategy under simultaneous forward test, including the ones that genuinely work. Nothing here is made easier to pass. What is opened is the right to ASK again, and the price of asking is charged in full, to everyone, at the moment of asking.

WHAT THIS WILL LOOK LIKE, SAID BEFORE IT HAPPENS SO IT CANNOT BE PRESENTED AS A DISCOVERY. Reopening buried compositions and draining queues will produce MORE registrations and, at any fixed bar, more survivors. That is arithmetic about the denominator and the count, not evidence of edge, and it must not be reported, presented or sold as one. The honest description of a generation that returns re-trial survivors is that a composition previously excluded from testing was tested on data that did not exist when it was buried. The forward record is what distinguishes the two, and only after it exists.

RATCHET POSITION. This note LOOSENS, without qualification, and it is declared as one. Two mechanisms that could exclude a proposed edge without a gauntlet verdict are removed, none is added, and no threshold is tightened anywhere to compensate. The house rule is that a protocol may tighten freely and any loosening requires the evidence and the argument on this chain BEFORE the results it would affect exist, and that is the whole reason this note is written first: the argument is above, the evidence is that the two mechanisms are demonstrably not statistical instruments (the fingerprint guard is a de-duplication rule, and the sibling cap is a per-run capacity bound whose value, 60, was never derived from anything), and no re-trial registration and no queue entry exist anywhere on the chain as this is chained. In the other direction the same note TIGHTENS in one respect, by declaring that a composition matching a quarantine or live registration is excluded with no expiry at all; that tightening needs no justification and is recorded only so the net effect is legible. The standing rule is unchanged.
