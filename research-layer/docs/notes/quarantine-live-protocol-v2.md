quarantine-live-protocol-v2: Amends quarantine-live-protocol-v1, chained at registry entry 2515, in one thing: the graduation arm. From the first live-gate run after this note is chained, a strategy graduates only when its forward record beats zero, as v1 requires, AND beats holding its own asset over the same days. The kill arm is v1's, word for word. Everything else in v1 stands as written, and the v1 note stays in force: the cohort rule, what live means and what it does not authorise, and the reasoning v1 records. This note edits no v1 text. It changes no existing verdict and moves no strategy by itself. Pre-declared before any strategy graduates under it: when this note was written, on 2026-11-05, the chain held no live_gate verdict.

THE KILL ARM IS v1's, WORD FOR WORD. The v1 paragraphs headed THE KILL ARM and THE KILL ARM CANNOT FIRE ON ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE govern every v2 assessment exactly as written: the forward equity series, the cone rebuilt at the matched forward trade count, the burial line at its first percentile, and the minimum of closed forward trades below which no burial occurs. The kill arm runs first, as in v1, and a strategy it buries is not counted in the graduation cohort.

THE GRADUATION P-VALUE. The hold test runs only on the eligible survivors of the kill pass: the strategies whose forward record has reached the eligibility length and that the kill arm left in quarantine. A strategy not yet eligible, or buried, gets no hold test. For each survivor two one-sided p-values are computed. p_zero is 1 minus the PSR of v1's graduation arm, unchanged: against a benchmark Sharpe ratio of zero, with the record's own skew and kurtosis and the full count of forward observations. p_hold is the p-value of the paired test below, against the forward hold. The strategy's p-value is p = max(p_zero, p_hold). This is an intersection-union test: a strategy graduates only when both nulls are rejected, and the test is valid with no assumption about how the two are correlated. The Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at q = 0.05 then runs over the survivors' p-values exactly as v1's cohort rule says, with p in the place of 1 minus PSR, and the strategies it rejects graduate. A book that is flat for its whole forward record has exactly constant forward returns, so its p_hold is 1.0, its p is 1.0 and it cannot graduate. v1's statement that a record with no trades cannot clear the graduation arm stays true for it.

THE FORWARD HOLD. It is built from the quarantine rows themselves, with no new data feed and no new file. Each row records the asset's price at the day's last bar (for an intraday cell, the cell named ASSET@TF, the book at the day's last bar). Per asset, the hold level on a date is that row's price divided by the price on the asset's earliest-dated row, so the level is 1.0 on the first forward row. Assets are combined by the mean of their levels, only on the dates where every asset of the strategy's rows reports; a date missing an asset is dropped, the same date rule as v1's forward equity series. No cost is charged, as no extra entry cost is charged to the forward record itself. The hold's daily returns are each level divided by the previous level, minus 1, so they start on the second forward day, as the strategy's do: the strategy's series is v1's, the daily returns of its forward equity series, and has no return for the first forward row. A step whose earlier strategy equity is not positive is dropped from the strategy's series, as in v1. The two series are paired position by position.

THE PAIRED TEST. H0: the strategy's Sharpe ratio is at most the hold's, one-sided, on the paired daily return series. Method: the studentized circular block bootstrap of the Sharpe-ratio difference of Ledoit and Wolf (2008), robust to fat tails and autocorrelation. Write s for the strategy's returns, h for the hold's, T for their common length, y_t = (s_t, h_t, s_t^2, h_t^2), and m = (m1, m2, m3, m4) for the mean of y over the record. The statistic is the per-period Sharpe-ratio difference f = m1 / sqrt(m3 - m1^2) - m2 / sqrt(m4 - m2^2): the variances carry no degrees-of-freedom correction. Its standard error se is the Ledoit-Wolf block estimate with block length B = HOLD_BLOCK_LEN. The record is cut from its start into floor(T / B) non-overlapping blocks, and the last T mod B observations are not used for se. For block j, psi_j is the sum over the block of (y_t - m), divided by sqrt(B). Then se = sqrt(mean over j of (g . psi_j)^2 / T), where g is the gradient of f with respect to m. The observed statistic is t_obs = f / se. The bootstrap draws HOLD_TEST_PATHS resamples. Each takes ceil(T / B) block starts, uniform on 0 to T - 1. Every block is the B consecutive positions from its start, taken modulo T (circular). The blocks are concatenated and cut to length T, and each pair (s_t, h_t) moves together. For each resample, f* and se* are computed by the same formulas, centred on the resample's own means, and t* = (f* - f) / se*. Then p_hold = (1 + the number of resamples with t* at or above t_obs) / (HOLD_TEST_PATHS + 1).

THE CONSTANTS AND THE SEED. HOLD_BLOCK_LEN = 5, HOLD_TEST_PATHS = 2000 and SR_DIFF_TOL = 1e-9 are declared in pipeline/beats_hold.py and change only by a new chained note. The random numbers come from numpy's default_rng(seed). All block starts are drawn in one call, integers(0, T, size=(2000, ceil(T / 5))), and row k of the result gives resample k. The seed is int(sid, 16) % 2**31 when the strategy id sid is a hexadecimal string, else 0, the same seed v1's rebuilt cone uses. The same record and the same seed give the same p_hold.

WHEN p_hold IS 1.0. p_hold is exactly 1.0, cannot reject, with no resample drawn, when any of these holds: the strategy's and the hold's return series differ in length, which happens when an earlier strategy equity was not positive and a step was dropped from its series; the record is shorter than 2 x HOLD_BLOCK_LEN, 10 paired returns; either series is exactly constant, or has a sample variance (n - 1) that is not positive, or either variance in f (m3 - m1^2 or m4 - m2^2) is not positive; the absolute per-period Sharpe-ratio difference |f| is below SR_DIFF_TOL; se is not positive; or t_obs is not finite. Two series that cannot be told apart cannot reject. A degenerate resample counts against rejection: a resample in which either series is exactly constant, either variance in f* is not positive, se* is not positive or not a number, or t* is not finite counts as t* = +inf, at or above t_obs.

WHY SR_DIFF_TOL. A book that holds a constant fraction of its asset is pure beta: its daily returns are c times the hold's, c > 0, so its per-period Sharpe ratio equals the hold's and f is zero up to rounding. Its se is of the same order as that rounding, so t_obs is noise divided by noise, and without the tolerance its p_hold would be random. In a build fixture whose two series differed only by rounding (largest difference 3.3e-16), the p-values over 100 seeds were close to uniform, 3 of them at or below 0.05, and before the tolerance was added one such book graduated under v2 in a test. Without the tolerance, a book whose returns equal its hold's up to rounding would pass the hold test by chance about one assessment in twenty, and the gate assesses weekly. 1e-9 per period is about 2e-8 annualized, far below any economically meaningful Sharpe-ratio difference.

WHAT A v2 ASSESSMENT RECORDS. A live_gate verdict carries metrics.protocol quarantine-live-protocol-v2 and, beside v1's fields, p_zero, p_hold and p. A verdict that buries records p_zero, p_hold and p as null, because a buried strategy was never hold-tested. A graduation's state change gives quarantine-live-protocol-v2 pass as its reason. The dated assessment report adds p_hold, p, and the strategy's and the hold's annualized Sharpe ratios (the per-period Sharpe ratio, population standard deviation, times the square root of 365). Those two Sharpe ratios are recorded in the report only and decide nothing, and the PSR against zero stays a recorded column.

THE SWITCH. The switch is dormant until this note is on the chain. The live gate looks for the note once, at the start of each run: when a note's text starts with quarantine-live-protocol-v2: followed by a space, that run applies v2, dry runs included. Without such a note every output is v1's, exactly as before. A note chained during a run takes effect at the next run. A note that only mentions quarantine-live-protocol-v2 arms nothing, and a second copy of this note changes nothing. The v1 note stays in force: a run that is not a dry run still refuses to move any strategy while no quarantine-live-protocol-v1 note is on the chain, whether or not this note is chained. This note's first line does not start with quarantine-live-protocol-v1, so it cannot satisfy that check.

WHO IT COVERS. v2 is not retroactive: no verdict already chained is re-judged, and no gauntlet verdict changes. From the switch onward it applies at every assessment to every strategy in quarantine, whatever gauntlet protocol admitted it, including the strategies registered before their class had a recorded benchmark control and the legacy pooled BTCUSD and ETHUSD books, because the forward hold comes from each strategy's own quarantine rows.

THE HONEST COST: GRADUATION SLOWS. Graduation now needs both tests. Against zero, clearing PSR 0.95 on 60 days already needs an annualized Sharpe ratio near 4.14, as v1 states. The paired test against a correlated hold needs a Sharpe-ratio DIFFERENCE of a similar size; this is the design's estimate, stated before building, not a measurement. A timing strategy that closely tracks its asset may take years longer to graduate, and many never will. That is the rule doing its job: a strategy that cannot be told apart from holding its asset should not be the reason real money moves. It also removes a leniency of v1: a beta strategy in a rising market had a small p-value, and many small p-values at once make Benjamini-Hochberg more lenient for every member of the cohort. Under v2 such a strategy's p is at least its p_hold, which stays large while its record cannot be told apart from its hold's. A short or low-correlation strategy whose Sharpe ratio is positive but below its asset's hold Sharpe ratio does not graduate, even though it could diversify a book: the rule asks whether the strategy is better than holding this asset, not whether it adds something to a portfolio.

RATCHET POSITION. This note TIGHTENS the graduation arm and leaves the kill arm unchanged. p is never smaller than v1's 1 minus PSR, so no strategy that would not graduate under v1 graduates under v2. It loosens nothing. v1's standing rule is unchanged: a future protocol may tighten freely, and any loosening requires the evidence and the argument on this chain before the results it would affect exist.
