gauntlet-protocol-v6.2: Supersedes gauntlet-protocol-v6.1, chained at registry entry 68486, for the gauntlet verdicts judged once the gauntlet worker switches to it, in one thing: it adds a seventh gate, beats_hold. v6.2 is v6.1 plus gate seven. A strategy passes gate seven only when the Sharpe ratio of its out-of-sample daily returns is strictly higher than the Sharpe ratio of holding its own asset over the same days. The six v6.1 gates are computed exactly as before, keep their thresholds and their order, and keep their own fail reasons. Every verdict judged before the switch stands as it was labelled, and so does every state change those verdicts produced. Pre-declared before the first verdict labelled v6.2 exists: until this note is on the chain the worker judges with the v6.1 battery and labels its verdicts v6.1, exactly as before.

EVERYTHING CHAINED FOR v6.1 APPLIES TO v6.2 VERDICTS. The notes below are keyed on v6.1 prefixes, so the inheritance is stated here and not assumed. A verdict labelled v6.2 carries what gauntlet-protocol-v6.1 says a v6.1 verdict carries, plus the beats_hold record below, and leaves out what v6.1 says a verdict leaves out. These apply to v6.2 verdicts as written: gauntlet-protocol-v6.1 itself (the registry-wide statistics are written later, in a gauntlet_stats entry linked by verdict_entry_hash, and no gate reads them); gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-amendment-1, chained at registry entry 81246 (the floor on SR*, and the PBO null built for every family with a verdict needing statistics); gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-amendment-2, chained at registry entry 188277 (the effective trials count made per cell partition); gauntlet-protocol-v6.1-intraday-daily-marks, chained at registry entry 168621 (an intraday strategy is simulated per bar and measured on daily marks); the family-kill ban, verify_registry.py invariant 12 (from the v6.1 note's line onward no gauntlet verdict may carry pbo_family_kill true and no state change may give pbo_family_kill as its reason); and the statistics linkage, invariant 11 (a gauntlet_stats entry must point at an earlier gauntlet verdict of the same strategy labelled gauntlet-protocol-v6.1 or gauntlet-protocol-v6.2, at most one entry per verdict). The statistics job writes gauntlet_stats entries for v6.2 verdicts as it does for v6.1 verdicts, and the worker's orphan repair gives a v6.2 verdict without its state change the change it implies, as it does for v6.1, without re-evaluating it. A v6.2 verdict, like a v6.1 verdict, carries no trials_n, so the list of protocols the trials_n floor reads is unchanged.

GATE SEVEN, beats_hold: THE TWO SERIES. The cutoff is 2023-12-31, the train fence the other gates use, and every comparison with it is by date only, on the first ten characters of a bar's date. The strategy's series is its out-of-sample daily returns: its equity curve marked once per UTC day (the last mark of each date), each return dated by the later of its two marks, kept when that date is after the cutoff. A step whose earlier mark is not positive gives no return, so its date drops out of the pairing below. This is the series the sharpe_floor gate splits at the cutoff. The hold's series is built per asset from the strategy's own bars. The out-of-sample bars are the bars dated after the cutoff. The entry price is the open of the first of them, the first raw bar (on an intraday cell the first bar of the first out-of-sample day, not the open of that day's last bar). On each out-of-sample date the level is the close of that date's last bar divided by the entry price, times (1 - per_side), where per_side is commission_per_side plus slippage_ticks of the cost model of the strategy's class, read from cells.CLASSES at judgment time: one side of a round trip charged at entry. A strategy that trades more than one asset holds the mean of the per-asset levels, on the dates every asset has (equal capital at the start, no rebalancing), which is the engine's rule for combining equity. The last level is multiplied by (1 - per_side) once more: the exit side. The hold's first return is its first level minus 1, so it carries the entry cost and the move from the entry open to that day's close; each later return is the level divided by the previous level, minus 1. No cost is charged between entry and exit. When any of the strategy's assets has no bar after the cutoff, the hold is empty.

GATE SEVEN, beats_hold: THE COMPARISON. The two series are paired on dates: only dates present in both are used, and both Sharpe ratios are computed on those dates alone. Each Sharpe ratio is the sample mean divided by the sample standard deviation (n - 1 in the variance), times the square root of 365, the formula the sharpe_floor gate applies to the train window. A Sharpe ratio does not exist when there are fewer than 30 paired returns or the sample variance is not positive. The gate passes when both Sharpe ratios exist and the strategy's is strictly greater than the hold's; equal Sharpe ratios fail. When either does not exist the gate fails: absence of evidence does not pass a gate, protocol-v4's rule. An empty hold therefore fails the gate. It is a point comparison, with no margin and no statistical test, like the other gates: the out-of-sample window is years long.

PLACEMENT AND ORDER. beats_hold is evaluated after the six gates, from the same dated returns, so no six-gate value is recomputed. The v6.2 order is sharpe_floor, oos_negative, edge_decay, mc_p05, p_ruin, cost_stress, beats_hold. A verdict passes only when all seven pass. When one of the six fails, the verdict's reason is the first of the six that failed, as under v6.1, and beats_hold is still computed and recorded. beats_hold is reported as the fail reason only when all six passed and it failed. A failing verdict moves the strategy to the graveyard with its reason, and a passing one to quarantine, as under v6.1. The six-gate battery that the hand-run pipeline/gauntlet.py shares is not edited: beats_hold is applied after it, only in the v6.2 battery the gauntlet worker uses, whose order is declared as FAIL_ORDER_V62.

WHAT A v6.2 VERDICT RECORDS. metrics.protocol is gauntlet-protocol-v6.2 on every verdict the v6.2 battery judges. metrics.beats_hold is recorded, pass or fail, on every verdict whose class declares benchmark "self", with five fields: strategy_sharpe, hold_sharpe (each null when it does not exist), n_days (the count of paired dates), window ("oos") and basis, the class's existing basis string: for fx "price returns, carry excluded on both sides" (fx's hold is long the foreign currency, carry excluded), for crypto "price returns, staking/funding yield excluded on both sides", and for every other class "price returns, dividends excluded on both sides". metrics.benchmark_relative, the raw-return control of B1, is computed before gate seven and recorded exactly as under v6.1, and decides no pass or fail. As under v6.1, for a class that declares benchmark "self" the control refuses a candidate that trades more than one asset or has no bar after the cutoff: that candidate gets no verdict, stays queued, and the worker run exits 1. So the empty-hold rule and the multi-asset rule above define gate seven, but the gauntlet worker does not reach them today. The multi-asset hold is reached at graduation, under quarantine-live-protocol-v2. Raw return is not the decider anywhere in this gate.

ONLY THE STRATEGY'S OWN CELL. The gate reads the strategy's own out-of-sample returns and the bars of the asset or assets it trades: the same bars and the same date fence as its other gates, and the cost model of its class as cells.CLASSES holds it at judgment time (the other gates read the copy in the strategy's registered spec). It reads no sibling, family, cohort or registry-wide statistic. The hold of a strategy's own asset over its own window is a property of the strategy alone, so v6's founding principle, that every input to every gate is a property of the strategy alone, holds.

WHICH CLASSES. The gate applies to every class whose CLASSES entry declares benchmark "self": today crypto, fx, equity_etf, bond_etf and metal_etf. For a class with any other benchmark value the gate records nothing (no beats_hold field) and passes, because there is no own-asset hold to beat; that verdict is still labelled v6.2.

NOT RETROACTIVE, AND WHEN IT STARTS. v6.2 does not apply retroactively. No verdict is re-judged under it, and no strategy in quarantine, live or the graveyard is re-judged at the gauntlet. Every v6.1 verdict stands, including one written by a worker run that began before this note was chained. A strategy already in quarantine meets its own asset's hold only at graduation, and only under quarantine-live-protocol-v2, a separate note. The switch is dormant until this note is on the chain: the gauntlet worker reads the chain once at the start of each run, and when that read holds a note whose text starts with gauntlet-protocol-v6.2: followed by a space, that run judges every candidate with the v6.2 battery and labels its verdicts v6.2. Without such a note it judges with the v6.1 battery and its output is exactly the v6.1 output. A note chained during a run takes effect at the next run. A note that only mentions gauntlet-protocol-v6.2 arms nothing, and a second copy of this note changes nothing. The worker still refuses to write any verdict while no gauntlet-protocol-v6.1: note is on the chain.

DECLARED CONSEQUENCE. A short strategy, or one with low correlation to its asset, whose Sharpe ratio is positive but below its asset's hold Sharpe ratio fails gate seven, even though it could diversify a book. The rule asks whether the strategy is better than holding this asset, not whether it adds something to a portfolio. A portfolio-level test, such as alpha against the hold or a contribution to the whole-pool optimizer, answers a different question and is not part of this protocol.

RATCHET POSITION. This note TIGHTENS: it adds a gate, removes none, and moves no threshold, so a strategy that fails v6.1 also fails v6.2. More failures under v6.2 than under v6.1 are the gate doing its job, not a defect. The motivation was measured on the live chain on 2026-11-05, read-only: of the 894 strategies in quarantine, none of the 616 equity_etf and metal_etf strategies that carry a recorded B1 control beat its own asset's hold on raw out-of-sample return. That control is raw return at the strategy's own exposure, with the hold charged one round trip, so it motivates the rule and does not predict how many strategies gate seven will fail.
