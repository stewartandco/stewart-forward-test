gauntlet-protocol-v6.1: Supersedes gauntlet-protocol-v6 only in WHEN the registry-wide recorded statistics are written. Everything else in v6, chained at registry entry 2514, stands as written. Every verdict judged before this note stands, and so does every state change those verdicts produced. This is an addendum, not a new battery: no gate is added, removed or re-tuned, and no threshold moves. Pre-declared before the first verdict labelled v6.1 exists. The gauntlet worker refuses to write a verdict until this note is on the chain.

THE CHANGE, AND ONLY THE CHANGE. Under v6 every gauntlet verdict was written together with registry-wide statistics: the effective-trials count trials_n, the count of registered strategies registered_n, the deflated Sharpe, the observed PBO with its null percentile, plateau_ok and the Harvey-Liu haircut. Computing them means re-simulating and clustering every registered strategy. Under v6 not one of them gated, and v6.1 keeps it that way: no gate reads any of them. A verdict labelled v6.1 is written when the candidate's own six gates have run, carries metrics.protocol equal to gauntlet-protocol-v6.1, and does not wait for any of those statistics.

WHAT A v6.1 VERDICT CARRIES. Every per-strategy metric v6 recorded that is a property of the strategy alone: the in-sample and out-of-sample edge per trade and the raw edges, the volatilities, the edge decay, mc_p05_equity, p_ruin, cost_stress_net_pnl, train_sharpe, the era summary, the exit reasons, the purged walk-forward report and the regime split, the last two each still carrying their window key, and the other fields that describe the strategy alone. It does not carry deflated_sharpe, trials_n, registered_n, expected_max_sharpe, the trials alignment fields, any PBO field, pbo_family_kill, plateau_ok, haircut or sibling_group_n. sibling_group_n, the one group-level count v6 kept as provenance, is therefore no longer written to the verdict, and it is not part of the statistics entry either. The artifact bundle keeps the cutoff and the per-cell data_end exactly as before.

WHERE THE STATISTICS GO. A later chain entry of type gauntlet_stats records them, one per v6.1 gauntlet verdict, linked to that verdict by verdict_entry_hash. It carries trials_n, trials_n_raw, trials_n_floor, trials_n_floor_entry_hash, registered_n, deflated_sharpe, pbo, pbo_percentile, plateau_ok, haircut, cluster_method, data_vintage, data_digest and data_end_by_cell, together with the quantities the deflated Sharpe was computed from. It records and never changes a strategy's state. It is written by a separate job, and it may follow its verdict by days. No gate, state change or promotion waits on it. verify_registry.py rejects a gauntlet_stats entry that does not point at an earlier v6.1 gauntlet verdict of the same strategy, and a second entry for one verdict.

WHAT A RECORDED STATISTIC DESCRIBES. A gauntlet_stats entry describes the registry and the price data as they stood at its own data_vintage, not as they stood on the day of the verdict. data_vintage is a date, the most recent Sunday on or before the run, and the job reads each price file truncated at that date. data_digest is the sha256 over the truncated bars of every cell the run read, and data_end_by_cell gives the last bar of each of the strategy's own cells. The vintage names a Sunday but does not pin the bars, because rows dated on or before that Sunday can still arrive during the week for some series, fx and equity ETF data among them. Two entries on one vintage can therefore rest on different data, and the digest is what tells them apart. registered_n counts every registered strategy at the run. The deflated Sharpe is computed over the return series v6 used, and cluster_method names the clustering method.

HOW trials_n IS RECORDED. The clustering method is unchanged from protocol-v3: k is the cluster count that maximises the silhouette quality score, the mean of the silhouette values divided by their standard deviation, over k from 2 to n minus 1, for the correlation-distance clustering of the daily-return series of every registered strategy, every buried one included. That argmax is recorded as trials_n_raw. The effective trials used for the recorded deflated Sharpe and for the Harvey-Liu haircut is trials_n, the larger of trials_n_raw and trials_n_floor. trials_n_floor is the highest cluster count already on this chain: trials_n on gauntlet verdicts under protocols v3, v4, v5 and v6, and trials_n_raw on earlier gauntlet_stats entries. Registration counts recorded under v2 are excluded. The entry that supplied the floor is named by trials_n_floor_entry_hash. The recorded effective trials therefore never falls as the registry grows. THE REASON IS A MEASURED FACT. On a registry that only grew, the argmax moved from 302 at 6,075 registered strategies to 480 at 11,476 and then to 44 at 11,835, and a falling N would make the recorded deflated Sharpe more lenient as more strategies had been tried. At the time of this note the highest value on the chain is 302, the trials_n of a v6 gauntlet verdict at registry entry 30782 (2026-09-03T21:21:06Z). trials_sr_var is computed from the current clustering and is not floored. Nothing here changes a gate: the deflated Sharpe and the haircut are recorded and do not gate.

THE FAMILY KILL IS NOT APPLIED. v6 states that the family kill is retired. The v6 implementation did not retire it, and five strategies were buried by it under verdicts labelled v6. That failure and its handling are recorded in the correction note chained with this one. From this note onward no gauntlet verdict applies the family kill, and verify_registry.py rejects any gauntlet verdict that carries pbo_family_kill true, and any state change that gives pbo_family_kill as its reason, at any chain line after this note. The five v6 burials before it stay valid chain history, and the verifier does not reach them.

GATES RETAINED, UNCHANGED, IN THIS ORDER: sharpe_floor at 0.4 on the train window, oos_negative, edge_decay above minus 25 percent on volatility-normalized per-trade edge, mc_p05 above 1.0, p_ruin below 0.05, cost_stress positive at double slippage. Six gates, each a property of the individual strategy and computable without reference to any sibling, any group or any registry-wide statistic. The screen is untouched: screen-protocol-v1 keeps its 2023-12-31 train fence, its floor of 40 trades and its requirement of net above zero after costs.

RATCHET POSITION. Against v6 as chained, this note changes nothing a gate decides, so it neither loosens nor tightens the battery. It changes when recorded numbers are written and which numbers a verdict itself carries. Against what the v6 implementation actually did, applying the six gates alone is a loosening for the five strategies the family kill buried, and the correction note says what happens to them. No threshold is tightened anywhere to compensate. The multiplicity warning in v6 is unchanged: more survivors than v5 produced would be evidence of a looser gate and not of edge, and the deflated Sharpe at 0.95 on the forward record remains the place where the search burden is priced.
