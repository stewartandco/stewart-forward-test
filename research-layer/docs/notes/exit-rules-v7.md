exit-rules-v7: The rule governing HOW A TRADE MAY CLOSE. It supersedes no earlier note of its own kind, because none exists: every exit the Composer has ever registered was inherited from block grammar v1 as a default, and no chained note has ever said which exits are permitted. It is not therefore a first statement on the subject. It MODIFIES behaviour that has been in force since the first registration on 2026-08-13 and that nearly every registration since has carried, and it says so plainly rather than resting on the absence of a note named after it. Pre-declared before any version-2 registration exists: verified at source on 2026-09-03 and again at chaining, every strategy_registered entry on this chain carries version 1, and the verifier rule this note arms has nothing to check until the first registration that follows it. Nothing on the chain is re-judged, no verdict moves, and no buried strategy is unburied.

WHAT IS ON THE CHAIN ALREADY, QUOTED. Block grammar v1 (commit d0b328a, 2026-08-06, "feat(composer): block grammar v1 with param validation") was chained by the Composer's first real run on 2026-08-13: entry 342 registered stop/pct_stop with the schema pct grid [0.05, 0.1, 0.15], and entry 344 registered exit/time_stop with the schema max_bars grid [10, 20, 40], both at 07:49:52Z, alongside the ten other v1 types chained by the same run between 07:49:47Z and 07:49:52Z. Neither was a decision. The design record for that grammar carries no argument for a calendar exit or for a fixed-percent stop; they were defaults. A forced exit lifts turnover, the screen's floor of 40 trades had killed 1,418 registrations by 2026-09-03, and 5,038 of 6,050 registrations carried the time stop; no chain entry records why any family kept it. Counted read-only from the live chain on 2026-09-03, through chain line 30630, the 6,050th registration: of those 6,050 registrations, 5,038 carry an exit/time_stop block (max_bars 40 on 2,695, 20 on 1,931, 10 on 412), 1,968 carry a stop/pct_stop block, and 1,265 carry an ma_cross, ma_cross_ds or ma_cross_dense entry. That third group closes trades by a path that is on NO chain entry at all: the engine's legacy branch exits an ma_cross* position at the next open once the fast average is back on the wrong side of the slow, writes exit_reason "signal", and no block_type_registered schema, no spec block and no note has ever declared it. 449 registrations carry none of the three. No verdict on this chain records how often any exit closed a trade, because verdict metrics carried no exit-reason counts until now.

WHAT IS UNTOUCHED, STATED FIRST SO IT CANNOT BE READ AS SOMETHING ELSE. Every verdict stands, unedited, forever. Every strategy_id keeps the lifecycle state it has, and no strategy_id ever returns. Every legacy registration keeps executing under the version-1 semantics it was registered with: the deadline exit, the fixed-percent stop and the implicit crossunder all still run for a version-1 specification, because the quarantine forward runner re-simulates every quarantined legacy strategy daily and their forward record must not change under them. The engine keeps that path as a separate branch, frozen by a golden captured before the engine was edited: three legacy fixtures covering every legacy exit path (time, stop, signal), replayed with equality required on every trade and on the final five points of the equity curve, no tolerance. Nothing is re-judged. Nothing is deleted, including the two retired block types themselves: chained schemas are immutable, so stop/pct_stop and exit/time_stop remain in the grammar and are refused by policy for new registrations, never edited.

THE RULE (D15, Coen, 2026-09-03). The only forbidden exit is the calendar. exit/time_stop is retired from the Composer grammar: time gating exits on the calendar, not the market, the same reason every Stewart and Co system was recalibrated off time gates. Valid exits, every one DECLARED per family: (1) a stop-loss whose level is indicator-placed, which is mandatory, and pct_stop, a fixed percent with no indicator, is retired; (2) an R-multiple target, optional; (3) indicator-event signal exits, optional, of which "crossover in, crossunder out" is the canonical example, and the SAME indicator as the entry is allowed for a signal exit. A registration made under this rule carries version 2. A version-2 specification has exactly one entry, at least one stop, at least one risk block, no retired type, an optional target, and zero or more exit blocks; the engine refuses a retired type under version 2 outright. Under version 2 there is no deadline and no implicit exit of any kind. A stop is a LEVEL read from an indicator at the signal bar (the ATR stops, or the new swing_stop, ma_stop, channel_stop and band_stop), fixed at entry with no trailing; a level not strictly on the adverse side of the entry makes the signal ineligible, no trade, counted as stop_invalid rather than skipped silently; with several stops the tightest adverse level wins. A declared signal exit (ma_crossunder, channel_exit, zscore_revert, tstat_decay, regime_flip) is evaluated on close t and filled at open t+1 exactly as an entry is, and only after the barrier checks at that bar, in this order: gap through stop, gap through target, intrabar stop, intrabar target, then the signal. Every closed trade records why it closed, as stop, target, or signal with the exit type named, and an open position at sample end is never a trade; it is marked to market and reported as open. Those counts are recorded on every screen and gauntlet verdict from here on and are read by no gate. THE VERIFIER RULE, armed by this note: from the first registration after the note entry whose text begins "exit-rules-v7:", every strategy_registered must carry version 2 and no block whose role and type are retired, and every block type must still be chained. Registrations before the note stand as version 1; the walk is in chain order, so nothing earlier can trip a rule armed later, and a later note that merely mentions this rule does not arm it a second time.

THE DISPOSITION (D15(b), disposition A). Everything is re-trialled under the new grammar inside the unified re-run that D8 pre-declares and D9 governs. Nothing is deleted and old verdicts stand as history. A legacy registration whose engine behaviour is UNCHANGED under this rule, meaning it carries no retired type and its entry is not ma_cross*, is declared compliant as it stands and is NOT re-registered; its fingerprint is recorded in both forms so that a version-2 copy of the same composition is refused as the duplicate it is. Every other legacy composition is to be proposed again through the Composer with a declared exit set, as a version-2 specification, and enters the queue as a D9 re-trial: a NEW strategy id and a new number, carrying none of the old record. The composition fingerprint carries the version, so a re-trialled block list under version 2 is a different trial from its version-1 ancestor and does not collide with it; the only version-2 fingerprints that collide are the ones recorded in both forms above, and every version-1 fingerprint already on the chain is unchanged. The price is the one family-openness-v1 charges and it is charged in full: each re-trial is a strategy_registered entry, so it enters registered_n and the effective-trials clustering behind trials_n, the deflated Sharpe on every verdict computed after it is harder to clear, and a survivor joins the quarantine cohort that Benjamini-Hochberg divides q by. Firing the re-trial is a chain write inside a pipeline cycle and is Coen-gated; the classification of which registrations stand and which are re-trialled is a report and writes nothing.

WHAT THIS WILL LOOK LIKE, SAID BEFORE IT HAPPENS SO IT CANNOT BE PRESENTED AS A DISCOVERY. Trade counts will FALL. A forced exit every 40, 20 or 10 bars manufactured turnover, and 5,038 of the 6,050 counted above had one; without it a family's trade count is whatever its declared entry and exit signals produce over the training window. The screen's floor of 40 trades does not move. More families failing at trade_count is the honest consequence and is not a defect in the gate: the Composer must find turnover through the entry and exit signals it declares, not through the calendar. Any survivor count under this rule is NOT comparable to any count under gauntlet-protocol-v6 as it stood, in either direction. Fewer survivors is not evidence that the edges weakened; it is evidence that a mechanism which lifted trade counts stopped running. Survivors that the re-trial returns are arithmetic about the denominator, exactly as family-openness-v1 already says, and must not be reported, presented or sold as edge. What the chain gains that it has never had is the exit-reason count on every verdict, and the honest reading of it is that it says how a strategy's trades closed, not whether the strategy works.

RATCHET POSITION. This note TIGHTENS, without qualification. A whole class of exits is forbidden. A stop with no indicator behind it is forbidden. An exit path that ran on no chain entry is closed, and every exit must now be declared in the specification the chain holds. No threshold moves anywhere: the screen's floor of 40 trades, the six gates of gauntlet-protocol-v6 in their order, the deflated Sharpe at 0.95 and the quarantine-live protocol are exactly as they were. The nine new block types are additions to the space the Composer may search, judged by the unchanged battery, and an addition to what may be TRIED is not a loosening of what must be PASSED. In the other direction the same note carries one asymmetry, recorded so the net effect is legible: legacy strategies in quarantine keep running under a rule this note forbids for new registrations. That is accepted for the reason the quarantine-standard-asymmetry note (registry entry 2308) gave for a different set of strategies, that re-judging a closed or running record under a later standard is the failure mode this chain exists to prevent, and it does not become acceptable when the later standard is stricter. The standing rule is unchanged.
