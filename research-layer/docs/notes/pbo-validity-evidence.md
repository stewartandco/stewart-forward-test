pbo-validity-evidence: EVIDENCE PUBLISHED UNDER THE RATCHET, BEFORE ANY CHANGE TAKES EFFECT AND BEFORE THE RESULTS IT WOULD AFFECT EXIST. The gauntlet-protocol-v4 note's standing rule is that a future protocol may tighten freely and any loosening requires the evidence and the argument on this chain before the results it would affect exist. This note is that evidence, for a change to the pbo gate that would run in the permissive direction. It reopens nothing. Generation 4's 19 gauntlet failures and the 88 strategies buried before them stay buried, on the same principle that kept generations 1 through 3 buried when their protocols were superseded, and the three strategies in quarantine keep their protocol-v3 verdicts under the quarantine-standard-asymmetry note. Every measurement below was produced by a write-free diagnostic reading committed artifacts and this chain, changing no gate and no threshold.

THE DEFECT, WHICH IS ANALYTIC RATHER THAN STATISTICAL. The CSCV implementation computes omega as the out-of-sample rank of the in-sample winner divided by n_configs plus one, and counts a split as overfit when omega is less than or equal to 0.5. That is Bailey, Borwein and Lopez de Prado's own convention and the code documents it as such: the median out-of-sample rank counts as overfit rather than as a pass. The convention is harmless at the family sizes that literature assumes, where the boundary case has vanishing measure. At small ODD n_configs it is not harmless, because the median rank lands exactly on omega equals 0.5 and is swept into the overfit count. Under the idealised null in which the winner's out-of-sample rank is uniform, the expected pbo is therefore 0.5 exactly when n_configs is even, and n_configs plus one over two n_configs when it is odd. For a family of 5 that is 0.600. For a family of 3 it is 0.667. For 25 it is 0.520. The protocol's family-kill line is 0.50. At a family of 5 the no-skill expectation therefore sits ABOVE the line at which this protocol declares that the selection process is actively harmful.

WHICH FAMILIES THIS TOUCHES, AUDITED ACROSS EVERY SIBLING GROUP ON THE CHAIN. There are 18 sibling groups. The 12 registered before generation 4 have sizes 4, 4, 4, 4, 4, 6, 6, 8, 8, 8, 12 and 12. Every one is EVEN, so every one carries a uniform-rank null of exactly 0.500 and none is affected by the parity defect. All 6 generation-4 groups have exactly 5 members, so every one carries a null of 0.600. Generation 4 is the first generation in this pipeline's history to land on an odd family size, and it did so on every family it produced.

THE CAUSE IS INTERNAL TO PROTOCOL-V4 AND IS A COLLISION BETWEEN ITS TWO NEW GATES. The plateau gate requires a registered sibling one step below and one step above a candidate on every swept axis, so a three-value sweep leaves only its middle point eligible and a five-value sweep leaves three. The Composer's system prompt encodes exactly that reasoning, instructing it to prefer one or two well-motivated axes at FIVE contiguous values because five values leave three eligible candidates where three leave one. Five is odd. The plateau rule therefore drove every generation-4 family onto the single arm of the pbo estimator where that estimator is biased upward, and the bias is large enough to cross the pbo gate's own kill threshold. The two gates added by the same protocol are in mechanical conflict, and nothing in the protocol's design or in its write-free ratchet diagnostic could have surfaced it, because the diagnostic ran only against the pre-existing even-sized families.

THE MEASURED NULL, ON REAL DATA. The idealised uniform-rank calculation above assumes away the real return distribution, the correlation between siblings and the conditioning induced by splitting one fixed series into halves. To measure the null without assuming any of that, a PERMUTATION null was constructed directly from the committed generation-4 equity curves: for each day, the sibling labels are randomly permuted. The daily cross-section is preserved exactly, so the real fat tails, the real common market factor and the real cross-sectional dispersion all survive, and only PERSISTENT sibling-specific skill is destroyed. That is precisely the null the pbo gate's semantics assume. Measured pairwise correlation among real generation-4 siblings runs from 0.544 to 1.000, with family means of 0.770, 0.837, 0.843, 0.897, 0.952 and 0.999, so this is not a low-correlation regime.

Over 100 draws for each of the 6 families, 600 draws in total, the mean of the family means is 0.599 against the analytic prediction of 0.600. The probability that a family with NO persistent skill differences at all is family-killed by the 0.50 line runs from 61 to 72 percent across the six. The probability that such a family passes the 0.20 line runs from 3 to 9 percent. A block-permutation variant that permutes labels in 21-day blocks, and so retains part of each sibling's own persistence, gives family means of 0.590 to 0.640 and a mean of approximately 0.62, confirming the result is not an artifact of destroying within-sibling autocorrelation.

WHERE GENERATION 4'S OBSERVED VALUES ACTUALLY SIT. Against its OWN permutation null, each observed value falls at the following percentile. symmetric_tstat_trend_ff, observed 0.689, 62nd percentile. symmetric_tstat_trend_voltarget, observed 0.803, 72nd percentile. short_only_fast_macross_high_n, observed 0.651, 50th percentile. symmetric_macross_stop_geometry, observed 0.761, 71st percentile. below_ma_short_regime_trend, observed 0.873, 83rd percentile. breakout_vol_state_filter, observed 0.030, 0th percentile. Not one family reaches the 90th percentile, let alone the 95th. The two families that carried the nine strategies this gate killed sit at the 62nd and 72nd percentile of what the same family produces with no skill differences whatsoever. Against a calibrated null, generation 4 shows no statistically significant evidence of overfitting in any family.

A SECOND DEFECT, INDEPENDENT OF PARITY: THE ESTIMATOR IS TOO DISPERSE FOR THE THRESHOLDS APPLIED TO IT. The standard deviation of a single family's pbo across the permutation draws is approximately 0.24 at n_configs 5, and the repository's own test suite already documents approximately 0.23 at n_configs 8 with s equal to 8, where it records that a third of seeds fall outside the interval 0.25 to 0.75 and that a single-seed assertion would therefore prove nothing about the estimator. The gate's entire pass band, from 0 to 0.20, is therefore narrower than one standard deviation of the quantity being thresholded. This is not a parity effect and would survive a parity fix untouched. It applies to the even-sized historical families as well, which means the write-free diagnostic reported in the protocol-v4 note, that pbo would have killed half the families ever registered, rests on single-draw estimates of a statistic with that dispersion. That diagnostic's kills are not disturbed by the parity finding, since those families were even, but neither are they established as significant, because no per-family null was computed for them.

WHAT THIS EVIDENCE DOES NOT ESTABLISH. It does not establish that the nine strategies killed on pbo had genuine edge. They may be overfit; the finding is that this gate at this family size could not have distinguished the two cases, and a corrected gate is as likely to bury them again as not. It does not establish where the thresholds belong, only that a fixed threshold is the wrong instrument for a statistic whose null moves with family size, family size parity, sibling correlation and series length. It does not establish that breakout_vol_state_filter at the 0th percentile is a good family: its siblings are 0.999 correlated, meaning the swept filter barely changed the strategy, so its low pbo reflects the persistence of a tiny difference in a degenerate family rather than a robust edge, and every one of its members had already died at the screen on trade count. The family-size sweep run alongside this, at n_configs 4, 5, 8, 12 and 25 on synthetic correlated series, returned 0.518, 0.563, 0.536, 0.486 and 0.516 with 50 draws each; n_configs 5 is the highest as predicted, but those gaps are under two standard errors and that sweep is reported as consistent with the parity result rather than as independent support for it. The parity result rests on the analytic derivation, which is exact, and on the 600-draw real-data null that reproduced it to three decimal places.

THE DIRECTION DECIDED, WHICH TAKES EFFECT FOR GENERATION 5 ONWARD AND IS NOT APPLIED TO ANY EXISTING VERDICT. Two changes, to be specified and built under their own pre-declared protocol note before any generation is judged under them. First, the boundary tie is to be counted as a half event, so that omega exactly equal to 0.5 contributes one half rather than one to the overfit count, which makes the estimator unbiased at every family size and removes the parity defect at its source. Second, and the substantive change, the fixed 0.20 and 0.50 lines are to be replaced by a test of the observed pbo against the family's OWN permutation null, computed by the procedure described above, so that the gate becomes a hypothesis test whose reference distribution is calibrated to that family's size, parity, correlation and length rather than assumed. The cost is roughly one hundred additional CSCV evaluations per family, about two minutes, which is negligible against what a generation already spends. Neither change is drafted here; this note records the evidence and the direction so that both are on this chain before the protocol that implements them, and long before any result it would affect exists.

RATCHET POSITION. The change foreshadowed here runs in the permissive direction, because families that protocol-v4 killed would not necessarily be killed under it. It is therefore treated as a LOOSENING and carries the full burden that the standing rule imposes on one, regardless of whether correcting a demonstrable bias would also be defensible as a repair. That burden is discharged by publishing this evidence now, before the successor protocol is written and before generation 5 exists. The generation-4 per-gate breakdown promised by the protocol-v4 note was published first and separately, at registry entry 2503, deliberately carrying no argument, so that the breakdown could not be read as having been shaped by the remedy that followed it.
