gauntlet-protocol-v6: Supersedes v5 for all FUTURE verdicts. v1, v2, v3, v4 and v5 verdicts stand. All 107 previously graveyarded strategies remain buried, which is terminal, and that expressly includes the 7 buried as sibling_not_selected under protocol-v3, which this protocol would not bury today; the composition-fingerprint guard still blocks re-registration of any of them; the three strategies in quarantine keep their protocol-v3 verdicts under the quarantine-standard-asymmetry note. Pre-declared before any generation-5 specification exists and before any strategy has been evaluated under it.

THE PRINCIPLE THIS PROTOCOL ENCODES, stated by Coen on 2026-08-21: each individual edge needs to be tested, regardless of how similar it is to another, and EVERY INDIVIDUAL EDGE MUST BE STANDALONE WHEN RUNNING THROUGH THE GAUNTLET. A configuration is a candidate in its own right. It is judged on its own evidence, and its resemblance to a sibling is not evidence about it. Three mechanisms in v4 and v5 decided a strategy's fate on something other than its own performance, and this protocol removes all three from the gate battery while keeping every one of them as a recorded number.

STANDALONE IS A CHECKABLE PROPERTY, NOT AN ASPIRATION, AND IT IS THE INVARIANT THIS PROTOCOL IS ACCOUNTABLE TO. After the three removals below, every input to every remaining gate is a property of the strategy alone: its own in-sample and out-of-sample trades, its own daily returns, its own train-window Sharpe, and its own trades re-run under doubled slippage. No gate reads a sibling, a group, a neighbour, a grid position or a family statistic. One group-level quantity survives in the recorded metrics, sibling_group_n, and it is worth stating that it was never read by a gate even under v4 and v5; it is provenance, not judgment. A future amendment that reintroduces any group-level input to the battery contradicts this protocol's founding principle and needs its own pre-declared note saying so in those terms.

CHANGE 1, SELECTION IS RETIRED. Under v3 the gauntlet promoted the single highest deflated Sharpe per sibling group; under v4 and v5 it promoted the single strongest neighbourhood floor. Under v6 every strategy that passes the gate battery proceeds to quarantine. select_survivors no longer selects, the sibling_not_selected transition is retired for future runs, and a sibling group ceases to be a competition. The cost of the retired rule is measurable rather than hypothetical: 7 strategies passed every gate on their own evidence and were graveyarded because a similar sibling scored higher, all of them in generation 3, which produced 10 gauntlet passers and quarantined 3.

CHANGE 2, THE PBO GATE BECOMES A RECORDED NUMBER. CSCV is still computed exactly as protocol-v5 computes it, with the boundary tie as a half event, over distinct configurations, against the family's own permutation null, and the observed value, the null percentiles, the distinct count and the draw count are all still written to every verdict. It no longer gates. pbo and pbo_underpowered leave FAIL_ORDER, and the family kill is retired. Two reasons, both already on this chain. First, PBO is a statement about a SELECTION PROCEDURE applied to a family, not about whether one configuration's edge is real, so under the principle above it cannot bury a configuration that passed its own gates. Second, the power evidence chained at registry entry 2513 measured what it can resolve: a planted advantage of 0.75 annualized Sharpe over siblings is detected every time and 0.50 most of the time, while the winner's margin over its nearest rival in generation 4's six families was 0.000, 0.010, 0.058, 0.059, 0.097 and 0.103. A gate that needs roughly 0.50 and is handed 0.10 at best is not resolving the thing it is being asked to decide. It should report, and this protocol has it report.

CHANGE 3, THE PLATEAU GATE BECOMES A RECORDED NUMBER. Neighbourhood qualification is still computed and the outcome still recorded, but it no longer gates and no longer selects. This is the change that sits least comfortably, and it is stated plainly rather than smoothed over: the plateau rule was adopted from the trading-systems SOP, whose Phase 4 forbids selecting the best single configuration and requires selection by neighbourhood quality, and it was ratified by Coen on 2026-08-18 with its both-sides neighbour requirement accepted deliberately. v6 does not claim the SOP is wrong. It observes that the plateau rule judges a candidate by its NEIGHBOURS' scores and by its position on a grid, which the principle above does not permit, and that with selection itself retired by change 1 there is no longer a slot for the rule to allocate. THIS IS A DECLARED DIVERGENCE FROM THE SOP, larger than the purged-walk-forward divergence v4 declared, and it means a research-layer candidate and a trading-systems candidate no longer face the same named standard. Protocol-v4 existed to make those two standards identical. That reconciliation is hereby partially undone, deliberately, and any claim that both pipelines clear one bar must stop being made until it is repaired.

GATES RETAINED, UNCHANGED, IN THIS ORDER: sharpe_floor at 0.4 on the train window, oos_negative, edge_decay above minus 25 percent on volatility-normalized per-trade edge, mc_p05 above 1.0, p_ruin below 0.05, cost_stress positive at double slippage. Six gates, every one of them a property of the individual strategy and computable without reference to any sibling. That is the whole battery under v6. The screen is untouched: screen-protocol-v1 keeps its 2023-12-31 train fence and its floor of 40 trades and net above zero after costs. Harvey-Liu haircut, purged walk-forward and the regime split remain recorded and not gating, each still carrying its window key.

WHY THIS COSTS NOTHING STATISTICALLY, WHICH IS THE LEAST OBVIOUS PART. Promoting every passer rather than one looks like it should inflate the multiple-testing burden. It does not, because the trials denominator has never counted promotions. registered_n is len(all_specs) and trials_n is the effective-trials clustering over every registered strategy including every buried one; generation 4's verdicts record trials_n of 2 clusters over registered_n of 110. Promoting five siblings instead of one leaves both numbers exactly where they were and leaves the deflated Sharpe unchanged. The one-winner-per-group rule was therefore never carrying the multiple-testing load that its presence implied. It was a capacity decision about how many strategies enter forward testing, presented as a statistical one, and this protocol stops presenting it that way.

WHERE MULTIPLICITY IS NOW PRICED, AND THE RISK THAT CREATES. The deflated Sharpe at 0.95 remains the quarantine-to-live gate, computed on the forward record with N over all registrations, exactly as protocol-v3 placed it. After v6 that is the ONLY place the search burden is priced. That gate has never been calibrated and cannot bind for 60 trading days, and it is now load-bearing in a way it was not before. This protocol loosens the gauntlet while the gate downstream of it remains uncalibrated. That is recorded here, before any result, as the principal risk v6 takes on.

STATED BEFORE ANY RESULT EXISTS, AND THIS IS THE PART A LATER READER SHOULD HOLD THIS CHAIN TO. Removing three rejection mechanisms will produce MORE survivors. A generation returning ten or twenty quarantine entries is now plausible where v5 returned none, and generation 3 would have returned 10 rather than 3. THAT WOULD NOT BE EVIDENCE OF EDGE. It would be evidence of a looser gate, and it must not be reported, presented, or sold as a breakthrough. The honest description of a v6 generation that produces survivors is that a protocol which previously discarded them stopped doing so. The forward record in quarantine is what can distinguish the two, and only after it exists.

WHAT IS KNOWINGLY GIVEN UP. v4 and v5 added five rejection mechanisms between them and v6 keeps one of them, the Sharpe floor. The gauntlet's discriminating power now rests entirely on six per-strategy gates, and generation 4 demonstrated that a family with no selectable internal structure whatsoever can pass all six: the nine strategies that died at v4's PBO gate had cleared every one of them. Under v6 those nine would have reached quarantine. Whether that is right depends entirely on whether their forward records justify it, which is precisely the question quarantine exists to answer and precisely the question no gauntlet gate can answer in advance.

RATCHET POSITION. This protocol is a LOOSENING and is declared as one without qualification. Three rejection mechanisms are removed, none is added, and no threshold is tightened anywhere to compensate. The standing rule requires the evidence and the argument on this chain before the results a loosening would affect exist. The evidence was chained first and separately: the generation-4 per-gate breakdown at entry 2503, the PBO validity evidence at entry 2511, and the PBO power evidence at entry 2513, the last of which measured the gate's resolution against the separation real families actually exhibit. This note is the argument, and it exists before any generation-5 specification has been composed. Nothing here is applied to any existing verdict, and the rule keeping 107 strategies buried is untouched, including the 7 this protocol would not bury today. That asymmetry is the same one the quarantine-standard-asymmetry note declared for a different set of strategies, and it is accepted here for the same reason: re-judging closed verdicts under a later standard is the failure mode this whole chain exists to prevent, and it does not become acceptable when the later standard is kinder.
