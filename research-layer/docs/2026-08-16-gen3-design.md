# Composer gen-3 + Gauntlet protocol-v3 — design spec (2026-08-16, rev 2)

Protocol-v3 relocates the deflated-Sharpe test rather than recalibrating it,
builds the quarantine phase it depends on, and lets buried ideas be
rediscovered by the funnel instead of resurrected by decree.

**This spec supersedes rev 1** (effective-trial clustering as a DSR fix).
Rev 1's clustering was built and works — it recovers all seven families from
return correlations alone at K=18 — but measurement against the real registry
showed it moves the hurdle only 1.86 → 1.72 annualized against a best-ever
achieved 1.42, so it would not have changed any outcome. That module is kept
and reused; the conclusion it was built to support was wrong.

## Why v3 exists

### The objective was mis-specified, not the statistic

The gauntlet's job is to identify strategies that are **robust, positive
expected value, and slow to decay** — good enough for one person to run and
potentially sell. Five of the six gates test exactly that:

| Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|
| positive EV out of sample | (a1) OOS net > 0 |
| decays slowly | (a2) vol-normalized edge decay > −25% |
| robust across resampled paths | (b) Monte Carlo P05 terminal > 1.0 |
| survivable to run personally | (c) P(ruin) < 5% |
| survives real execution | (e) OOS net > 0 at 2× slippage |

The sixth, DSR ≥ 0.95, answers a different question: *could a skeptical
outsider distinguish this from the luckiest of N coin flips?* That is a
publication-grade bar, and it is calibrated accordingly — the implied hurdle
reached 1.86 annualized Sharpe, exceptional-fund territory, to earn the right
to be **paper-traded**. The strictest statistical test in the pipeline was
gating the cheapest, most reversible decision in it.

### Why the implied hurdle was so high

`SR* = sqrt(V[SR]) × multiplier(N)`. At N=56 the multiplier is 2.32; the
dominant term is `sqrt(V[SR]) = 0.80` annualized — **the spread of our own
strategies' Sharpes**, which run −1.36 to +1.42.

| Which strategies were registered | spread | implied hurdle |
|---|---|---|
| all 56 (what we did) | 0.80 | 1.86 |
| best 30 only | 0.17 | 0.40 |

Registering our failures is what made the gate unpassable. The variance term
conflates *quality* dispersion with *luck* dispersion, so honest registration
of dead ideas — the core virtue of this project — inflates the bar for the
survivors. A system that punishes honesty is mis-specified.

### The correction is placement, not calibration

Multiple-testing discipline still protects the operator: picking the best of
56 backtests and trading it is exactly how a solo researcher loses money.
But **quarantine is a stronger defence than any statistical correction** —
paper-trading forward on bars that did not exist at selection time cannot be
gamed by search. So:

- the gauntlet asks *"is this robust and positive-EV?"* (the five gates),
- quarantine does the real out-of-sample work,
- the multiple-testing correction moves to **quarantine → live**, where real
  capital is at risk and genuinely fresh evidence exists to compute it on.

**No number is being chosen to let current strategies through.** `DSR ≥ 0.95`
is retained verbatim; only its inputs and position change. The live gate
cannot bind until a strategy completes a full quarantine period, so its
calibration is finalized before anything approaches it, pre-declared as
always.

Coen-approved decisions:

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Gauntlet gates | The five robustness gates; DSR removed from this stage |
| DSR | Relocated to quarantine → live, threshold 0.95 unchanged |
| Quarantine | Built for real: daily forward decisions, stated minimum, stated pass criteria |
| Buried strategies | Stay buried. No resurrection, no state-machine amendment |
| Rediscovery | Allowed via the funnel: fingerprint drops the colliding SIBLING, not the family |
| Grammar | Unchanged |

## 1. Gauntlet (`pipeline/gauntlet.py`) — protocol-v3

`PROTOCOL = "gauntlet-protocol-v3"`.

Gates, in fixed fail order: `oos_negative → edge_decay → mc_p05 → p_ruin →
cost_stress`. The `dsr` check is **removed from this stage**; the fail-order
list shortens accordingly.

`deflated_sharpe` is still **computed and recorded** in every verdict — it is
informational at this stage, not a gate — using the effective-trial
clustering built in rev 1 (`pipeline/cluster.py`, retained). Verdict metrics
therefore keep every SCHEMA key plus `trials_n` (= effective clusters K),
`registered_n`, `is_edge_raw`, `oos_edge_raw`, `is_vol`, `oos_vol`. Recording
it means a future auditor can see exactly what the retired gate would have
said about every strategy that passed without it.

Selection is unchanged: one quarantine slot per sibling group, ranked by DSR
descending, ties by lexicographically smallest `strategy_id`. DSR remains the
*ranking* statistic even though it is no longer a *gate* — among strategies
that are all robust, preferring the one least likely to be luck is right.

## 2. Quarantine (`pipeline/quarantine.py`, new)

Quarantine stops being a state label and becomes a daily forward test.

**Daily runner.** `python -m pipeline.quarantine --date YYYY-MM-DD` computes,
for every strategy in `quarantine` state, that day's decision from the same
`pipeline/engine.py` executors the screen and gauntlet use, and appends one
`quarantine_decision` entry per strategy to the registry:

```jsonc
{"strategy_id": "...", "date": "2026-08-17", "asset": "BTCUSD",
 "action": "hold|enter_long|enter_short|exit", "price": 63351.08,
 "position_frac": 0.2, "equity": 1.0234}
```

Idempotent per `(strategy_id, date)`: re-running a date is a no-op, so a
missed day can be backfilled without duplicating. Decisions are computed from
bars up to and including that date only — the runner refuses to compute a
decision for a date whose bar is not yet in `data/`.

> **Amendment, 2026-08-16 (implementation), Coen-approved.** Two points this
> section left underspecified, both surfaced by code review:
>
> **The uniqueness key is `(strategy_id, date, asset)`,** not
> `(strategy_id, date)`. The payload above already carries `asset`, and a
> two-asset universe emits two rows per day, so the three-part key is the only
> consistent reading of what this section describes.
>
> **A new `quarantine_data_snapshot` entry type is added**, chained once per
> date immediately before that date's decision rows, carrying
> `{date, data_sha256: {asset: hex}}`. The runner recomputes each strategy's
> whole book from the first bar every day — deliberately, because it makes a
> backfilled row provably identical to a live one — which means the identity of
> the price files is load-bearing. Without it, a re-fetch or vendor restatement
> of a CSV would silently change what a reproduction yields for **every**
> historical day, and an auditor would have no way to detect it. `screen.py`
> and `gauntlet.py` already record `data_sha256` per asset in their artifact
> bundles; this brings quarantine to the same standard. It also acts as a
> detector: re-running a date whose recorded hashes no longer match the files
> on disk is refused, not silently recomputed. `verify_registry.py` enforces
> that snapshot dates are unique and that every `quarantine_decision` is
> covered by an earlier snapshot naming its asset.

**Graduation review** is a separate command, not automatic. `--review` reports
each quarantined strategy against the pre-declared criteria and writes
nothing; graduating a strategy is a Coen-gated action exactly like every
other real run in this pipeline.

**Pass criteria (pre-declared, SCHEMA-aligned):** ≥ 60 trading days AND
realized per-trade edge within the P25–P75 cone of the gauntlet's Monte Carlo
projection for that strategy (already stored in each artifact's
`mc_summary.json`). Falling outside the cone is not automatically fatal —
below P25 with ≥ 60 days is a `graveyard` transition with reason
`quarantine_underperform`; above P75 is reported but does not fail.

**The live gate (specification, not yet calibrated).** Deflated Sharpe
computed on the **quarantine forward period only**, with N = the number of
strategies that have entered quarantine to date and V[SR] the variance across
those strategies' quarantine Sharpes. Threshold `DSR ≥ 0.95` unchanged. Early
on N is small and the implied hurdle is low — correct, because little was
shortlisted; it rises as more candidates are paper-traded. **This gate cannot
bind for at least 60 trading days**; its final form is chained as its own
pre-declared note before any strategy approaches it, and this spec does not
authorize a live transition.

## 3. Composer (`pipeline/composer.py`)

**Rule 7 changes from family-level to sibling-level.** Currently any expanded
sibling colliding with a previously-registered composition drops the whole
family. With 56 compositions already buried in a modest grammar space, a
12-sibling family is likely to collide on at least one member and die
wholesale — which would silently prevent the rediscovery this generation
depends on.

New behavior: colliding siblings are dropped individually; the family
survives with its remaining siblings; the run reports counts explicitly
(`family X: 12 expanded, 3 already registered, 9 new`). A family whose
siblings *all* collide is dropped entirely, as before. Within-run duplicate
siblings are still dropped, and the exact-composition guard is otherwise
unchanged: a buried strategy can never be re-registered.

This makes the guard a **robustness filter**. A rediscovered idea must come
back at a different parameterization; if the idea is real its neighbours work
too, and if only the exact buried point worked it was overfit and deserved
burying.

`SYSTEM_PROMPT` gains generation 2's measured outcomes as evidence (reversion
lost 46–66%; short-only fired 10–19 times in six and a half years; 12 of 18
held positive vol-normalized decay; sizing is stated as untested because all
18 gauntlet entrants were `vol_target`). The block grammar is unchanged.

## 4. SCHEMA and verifier

`SCHEMA.md` gains the `quarantine_decision` entry type and records that the
DSR gate moved from gauntlet to quarantine→live under protocol-v3. The
lifecycle state machine is **unchanged** — no new transitions, `graveyard`
stays terminal.

`verify_registry.py` gains one invariant: a `quarantine_decision` must
reference a strategy currently in `quarantine` state, and `(strategy_id,
date)` must be unique. Existing invariants are untouched, and the current
1,985-entry chain must still validate.

## 5. What is deliberately NOT changed

- The 56 buried strategies stay buried. No resurrection, no reinstatement, no
  amendment to terminal states.
- The fingerprint guard still blocks exact re-registration, permanently.
- The screen (`screen-protocol-v1`), the training fence, the block grammar,
  and the honesty guard are all untouched.
- `DSR ≥ 0.95` is unchanged as a threshold.

## 6. Sequencing

1. Build and test (offline; no chain writes).
2. Chain the `gauntlet-protocol-v3` note.
3. Composer gen-3 `--dry-run` → Coen's gate → real run.
4. Screen → Coen's gate → real run.
5. Gauntlet v3 → Coen's gate → real run. Survivors enter `quarantine`.
6. Quarantine daily runner begins; graduation review is a later, separately
   gated decision once ≥ 60 trading days exist.

## 7. Testing (offline)

- Gauntlet: the fail-order list no longer contains `dsr`; a strategy with
  DSR 0.01 that passes all five robustness gates **passes**; `deflated_sharpe`
  is still present and correctly computed in its verdict metrics.
- Composer: a family with 3 of 12 siblings colliding registers exactly 9 and
  reports all three counts; a family whose siblings all collide is dropped;
  within-run duplicates still dropped; a buried composition still cannot be
  registered.
- Quarantine: a decision is computed only from bars ≤ its date; re-running a
  date is a no-op; a decision for a non-quarantined strategy is refused;
  `--review` writes nothing; the P25–P75 cone comparison is hand-checked
  against a fixture `mc_summary.json`.
- Verifier: a `quarantine_decision` for a non-quarantined strategy fails; a
  duplicate `(strategy_id, date)` fails; the live 1,985-entry chain still
  validates.
- Regression: the rev-1 clustering module's 13 tests still pass unchanged.

## Out of scope

Cross-asset relative-value blocks. Grammar changes. Any live-capital
transition. Any change to the screen or the training fence. Any re-judging of
the 56 buried strategies, by any mechanism.
